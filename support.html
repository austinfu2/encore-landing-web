Support

Reso is built by an independent developer. If you're stuck or something isn't working right, get in touch — we read every email.

Logging concerts

How do I log a show I've been to?

Tap the search tab, find the concert by artist or venue, and tap the rating sheet. You can score performance, sound, crowd, and venue separately, or just leave an overall rating. Add a review, photos, and friends who were there.

I can't find a concert that happened years ago.

Reso covers 300,000+ concerts but doesn't have everything yet. Email us with the artist, date, and venue and we'll do our best to add it.

A concert in the app has wrong details.

Email us with the URL or a screenshot. We'll fix it.

Account

I forgot my password.

On the sign-in screen, tap "Forgot password" and follow the email instructions.

How do I delete my account?

Open the app, tap your profile, tap the settings cog in the top-right, scroll to the bottom, and tap "Delete Account." This permanently removes all your logs, ratings, reviews, photos and personal information. No longer have the app? See getreso.app/delete-account.

How do I export my ratings?

Settings → Export ratings. You'll get a CSV file you can save or share.

Reso Plus

Is Reso free?

Yes — all of it, right now. That includes what will later be Reso Plus: advanced stats (year-over-year, top venues, top genres, streaks, a world map of every show) and unlimited lists. Your Year in Review and exporting your ratings are free for everyone, always.

Will that change?

Reso Plus may become an optional subscription later. If it does, we'll say so in the app first, and everything you've logged stays yours.

Bugs and feedback

Something is broken.

Sorry about that. Send us an email with what happened, what you expected to happen, and what device/iOS version you're using. Screenshots help a lot.

I have a feature idea.

We'd love to hear it. Email us — Reso is shaped by what concertgoers actually want.

Still stuck?

We respond to every email, usually within a day or two.
