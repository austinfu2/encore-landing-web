Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 27, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how Encore ("we," "us," or "the app") collects, uses, and shares information about you when you use the Encore mobile application. We respect your privacy and aim to be transparent about what data we collect and why.

Information We Collect

Information You Provide

  • Account information: when you create an account, we collect your email address, username, display name, and password (stored as a one-way hash — we never see your actual password).
  • Profile information: optional details you add to your profile, such as a bio, country, avatar, and links to your social media profiles.
  • Content you create: concert logs, ratings, reviews, photos, lists, favorites, comments, and likes.
  • Communications: emails or messages you send to our support address.

Information Collected Automatically

  • Device information: device type, operating system, app version, language, and time zone.
  • Usage data: screens viewed, features used, and approximate frequency of use.
  • Crash and performance data: diagnostic information that helps us fix bugs.
  • Advertising identifiers: a mobile advertising identifier (Apple's IDFA or Google's Advertising ID) and related device data, used to deliver and measure ads on the free tier (see Advertising below). Encore Plus subscribers are not served ads.

Information from Third Parties

  • Sign in with Apple / Google: if you choose to sign in with these services, we receive your name and email address.
  • Subscription information: when you purchase Encore Plus, Apple and our subscription provider (RevenueCat) share transaction information with us, including subscription status and renewal dates. We do not receive your payment card or billing details.

How We Use Information

We use the information we collect to:

  • Provide, maintain, and improve the app and its features
  • Authenticate your account and keep it secure
  • Display your content (such as ratings and reviews) according to your privacy settings
  • Send transactional emails (password resets, important account notices)
  • Send push notifications you have opted in to
  • Process subscriptions and manage premium entitlements
  • Show ads to free-tier users, and — with your consent — measure and personalize them
  • Detect, investigate, and prevent fraud or abuse
  • Comply with legal obligations

How We Share Information

Public Content

Concert ratings, reviews, lists, and profile information you mark as public are visible to other Encore users and may appear in search results. You can change the visibility of individual logs at any time.

Service Providers

We use third-party services to operate the app. These providers process data on our behalf:

  • Railway — server hosting and database
  • RevenueCat — subscription management
  • Firebase (Google) — push notifications and crash reporting
  • Apple — App Store transactions and Sign in with Apple
  • Google — Sign in with Google
  • Google AdMob — advertising (free tier only)

Each provider has its own privacy policy. We share only the data needed for them to perform their service.

Legal Requests

We may disclose information if required to do so by law, court order, or other valid legal process, or if we believe disclosure is necessary to protect our rights, the safety of users, or the public.

What We Don't Do

We do not sell your personal information. Other than the ad serving described in Advertising above, we do not share your personal information with third parties for their own marketing. We do not use your content to train AI models.

Advertising

Encore displays ads through Google AdMob on the free tier. To serve ads, AdMob may collect and process device and usage information, including a mobile advertising identifier (Apple's IDFA or Google's Advertising ID), IP address, device type, and approximate location derived from IP.

If you are in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom, or Switzerland, we ask for your consent before serving personalized ads, and you can change or withdraw it at any time via Settings → Ad privacy settings. On iOS, we also ask for your permission to track (App Tracking Transparency) before using your advertising identifier. If you decline, we serve non-personalized ads, which still involve limited data collection for basic ad delivery, frequency capping, and fraud prevention.

Encore Plus subscribers do not see ads. For details on how Google processes this data, see Google's Privacy Policy and "How Google uses information from sites or apps that use our services."

Data Retention

We keep your account information for as long as your account is active. If you delete your account (via Settings → Delete Account), we permanently remove your personal information, logs, ratings, reviews, and uploaded content from our active databases within 30 days. Some information may remain in encrypted backups for up to 90 days before being permanently erased.

Your Rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the following rights:

  • Access: request a copy of the personal information we hold about you
  • Correction: update inaccurate information through your profile, or by contacting us
  • Deletion: delete your account and personal data via the app or by contacting us
  • Export: download your ratings as CSV via Settings → Export ratings
  • Withdraw consent: opt out of push notifications via Settings → Notifications; change or withdraw ad-personalization consent via Settings → Ad privacy settings (EEA/UK/Switzerland)

To exercise any of these rights, email us at hello@encoreapp.to.

Children

Encore is not directed to children under 13 (or under 16 in the European Economic Area). We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, contact us and we will delete it.

International Data Transfers

Encore is operated from Portugal. By using the app, you understand that your information may be transferred to and processed in countries other than your own, including the United States, where our service providers operate.

Security

We use reasonable security measures to protect your information, including encrypted connections (HTTPS), hashed passwords, and access controls. However, no system is perfectly secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of material changes via the app or by email. The "Last updated" date above will reflect the most recent revision.

Contact

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy or our data practices, contact us at hello@encoreapp.to.
