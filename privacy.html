Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 19, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how Reso ("we," "us," or "the app") collects, uses, and shares information about you when you use the Reso mobile app and our websites (getreso.app and app.getreso.app). We respect your privacy and aim to be transparent about what data we collect and why.

The short version: Reso shows no ads, doesn't track you across other companies' apps or websites, doesn't use advertising identifiers, and doesn't sell your data. Exporting your ratings is free for everyone.

Information We Collect

Information You Provide

  • Account information: when you create an account, we collect your email address, username, display name, and password (stored as a one-way hash — we never see your actual password).
  • Profile information: optional details you add to your profile, such as a bio, location, country, avatar, and links to your social media profiles.
  • Content you create: concert logs, ratings, reviews, rankings, photos and videos, lists, shows you want to see or are going to, check-ins, comments, and likes.
  • Communications: emails or messages you send to our support address.
  • Waitlist: if you join the waitlist on our website, your email address and the page you signed up from. We use it only to tell you when Reso launches.

Information You Choose to Share from Your Device

  • Location: if you allow it, the app uses your device's location to show concerts and festivals near you. It is sent to our server to answer that request and is not saved to your account.
  • Photos: if you allow it, you can pick a profile picture or photos and videos to add to a log. "Find your past shows" reads only the date and place your photos were taken, on your device, and sends those dates and places — never the photos themselves — to find the concerts they match. They are not saved.
  • Camera: used only when you take a profile picture.

You can turn these permissions off at any time in your device's settings.

Information Collected Automatically

  • Crash reports: when the app crashes or hits an error, a diagnostic report (device model, operating system, app version, and what went wrong) helps us fix it.
  • Push notification token: if you turn on notifications, a token that lets us deliver them to your device.
  • Anonymous counts: we count how many times a few things happen each day — for example, how many shows were shared, or how many times "Open in Reso" was tapped on our website. These are daily totals only: they contain no account, device, or IP address and can't be linked to you.
  • Website visits: our websites use Cloudflare Web Analytics, which counts page views without cookies. Like any web server, ours briefly process your IP address to deliver pages and protect against abuse.

We don't use third-party analytics or advertising SDKs to follow what you do in the app.

Information from Third Parties

  • Sign in with Apple / Google: if you choose to sign in with these services, we receive your name and email address.
  • Subscription information: if you purchase Reso Plus, Apple and our subscription provider (RevenueCat) share transaction information with us, including subscription status and renewal dates. We do not receive your payment card or billing details.

How We Use Information

We use the information we collect to:

  • Provide, maintain, and improve the app and its features
  • Authenticate your account and keep it secure
  • Display your content (such as ratings and reviews) according to your privacy settings
  • Show you which people you follow went to the same shows
  • Send transactional emails (password resets, important account notices)
  • Send push notifications you have opted in to
  • Process subscriptions and manage premium entitlements
  • Detect, investigate, and prevent fraud or abuse
  • Comply with legal obligations

Legal Bases (EEA, UK, and Switzerland)

We process your information to provide the service you signed up for (performance of a contract); for our legitimate interests in keeping Reso secure, fixing bugs, and understanding in aggregate how it is used; with your consent where we ask for it (such as location, photos, and push notifications), which you can withdraw at any time; and where the law requires it.

How We Share Information

Public Content

Concert ratings, reviews, lists, and profile information you mark as public are visible to other Reso users and on our website, and may appear in search results. You can change the visibility of individual logs, or make your whole account private, at any time.

Service Providers

We use third-party services to operate Reso. These providers process data on our behalf:

  • Railway — server hosting and database
  • Amazon Web Services — storage for photos, videos, and avatars (EU)
  • Cloudflare — website and API delivery, security, cookie-free website analytics, and forwarding mail sent to our address
  • Resend — sending account emails, such as password resets
  • Firebase (Google) — push notifications and app crash reports
  • Sentry — server error monitoring (configured not to collect personal information)
  • RevenueCat — subscription management
  • Apple — App Store transactions and Sign in with Apple
  • Google — Sign in with Google

Each provider has its own privacy policy. We share only the data needed for them to perform their service.

Legal Requests

We may disclose information if required to do so by law, court order, or other valid legal process, or if we believe disclosure is necessary to protect our rights, the safety of users, or the public.

What We Don't Do

We do not sell your personal information or share it with advertisers or data brokers. We do not show ads, use advertising identifiers (Apple's IDFA or Google's Advertising ID), or track you across other companies' apps and websites. We do not use your content to train AI models.

Data Retention

We keep your account information for as long as your account is active. If you delete your account (via Settings → Delete Account), we permanently remove your personal information, logs, ratings, reviews, and uploaded content from our active databases within 30 days. Some information may remain in encrypted backups for up to 90 days before being permanently erased. You can ask us to remove your waitlist email at any time.

Your Rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the following rights:

  • Access: request a copy of the personal information we hold about you
  • Correction: update inaccurate information through your profile, or by contacting us
  • Deletion: delete your account and personal data via the app or by contacting us
  • Export: download your ratings as CSV via Settings → Export ratings — free for everyone
  • Objection and restriction: ask us to stop or limit some processing
  • Withdraw consent: turn off push notifications in Settings → Notifications, and location or photo access in your device's settings

To exercise any of these rights, email us at hello@getreso.app. If you are in the EEA, you can also complain to a data protection authority — in Portugal, the Comissão Nacional de Proteção de Dados (CNPD), or the authority where you live.

Children

Reso is not directed to children under 13 (or under 16 in the European Economic Area). We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, contact us and we will delete it.

International Data Transfers

Reso is operated from Portugal. By using the app, you understand that your information may be transferred to and processed in countries other than your own, including the United States, where our service providers operate.

Security

We use reasonable security measures to protect your information, including encrypted connections (HTTPS), hashed passwords, and access controls. However, no system is perfectly secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of material changes via the app or by email. The "Last updated" date above will reflect the most recent revision.

Contact

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy or our data practices, contact us at hello@getreso.app.
